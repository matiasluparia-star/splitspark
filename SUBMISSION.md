# SplitSpark — Cycle III / Pay

SplitSpark helps friends, housemates, and travel groups reimburse one person after a shared expense. Enter the total in NIM, group size, and recipient address. SplitSpark divides the bill down to the smallest NIM unit and generates a separate link for each guest. Guests can review the exact amount and recipient before approving the transfer through Nimiq Pay. SplitSpark uses the official Mini App SDK to request native NIM payments and displays the transaction hash returned by the wallet.
